# ProcuraAI

AI-powered procurement decision-support platform for supplier analysis, risk prediction, document intelligence, and intelligent recommendations.

## Project Status

🚧 Currently under development.

## Business Problem

Procurement teams often manually process supplier quotations, compare supplier terms, review supplier history, assess supplier risk, search historical contracts, and prepare negotiation communications.

ProcuraAI aims to assist procurement teams by bringing these activities into an AI-powered decision-support workflow.

## Planned Capabilities

* Supplier quotation analysis
* Supplier comparison
* Supplier risk prediction
* Procurement document intelligence
* Historical contract and document search
* RAG-powered procurement knowledge retrieval
* AI-powered supplier recommendations
* Explainable recommendations
* Negotiation email generation
* Human-in-the-loop approval
